Global Financial Services Restructuring

How we redesigned the ethical framework for a Fortune 500 bank following serious regulatory action, restoring investor confidence and operational trust.

The Challenge

A leading multinational banking organization faced severe enforcement actions from dual-jurisdiction regulators over structural lapses in anti-money laundering (AML) controls and corporate trading culture. The bank's existing compliance framework was sprawling but largely ineffective—a classic "check-the-box" program that failed to identify systemic risks propagating through its middle management. The organization was suffering from crippling fines, severe reputational damage, and a collapse in shareholder trust.

Our Diagnostic Approach

Airetris GRC was brought in by the newly appointed Board of Directors to conduct a root-cause forensic review. Using the Areté Method, we stepped past standard policy audits and examined the actual incentive mechanisms and behavioral drivers present within the trading floors.

Our findings indicated that while the formal rules were sound, aggressive performance targets completely overshadowed compliance mandates, creating intense internal pressure that marginalized the compliance function.

Execution & Strategy

We designed and implemented a sweeping governance mandate structured around three core pillars:

  • Incentive Restructuring: Collaborating with the Compensation Committee to tie 30% of executive and senior trader bonuses directly to quantifiable ethical and compliance metrics, shifting the behavioral baseline.
  • Compliance Empowerment: Restructuring the reporting lines so that the Chief Compliance Officer (CCO) held a direct, unfiltered reporting lane to the Board’s Risk Committee, bypassing the CEO.
  • Cultural Reboot & Monitoring: Deploying an AI-assisted behavioral monitoring system that analyzed internal communications and transaction speeds for early-warning markers of high-pressure rule circumvention.

The Result

Over a 24-month engagement period, the bank transformed from an industry laggard in compliance to a recognized pioneer in behavioral risk management. The regulatory monitors formally endorsed the new framework, leading to an early exit from the consent order.

Key Impact Metrics

$320M
Reduction in annual compliance-related regulatory fines within the first two years of framework adoption.
94%
Employee awareness and engagement shift regarding ethical reporting lines (up from 41%).
18 Mo.
Time required to successfully exit the restrictive federal consent order.
